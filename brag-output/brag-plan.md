# Brag Plan: Mailloop

## Planning rubric
1. App: job seekers send personalized cold emails through their own Gmail.
2. Strongest claim: “Send up to 15 cold emails at once.” Each recipient receives a separate email.
3. Visual hook: violet-to-pink emphasis on 15, paired with a personal email preview.
4. Actual UI: Compose recipient shortlist, Personal preview, Attach Resume, Send queue; History status rows.
5. Shortest satisfying film: 20 seconds.
6. Tone: polished; “clean, professional product launch.”
7. Audio: steady, warm music with two quiet UI accents; Kokoro narration, explicitly requested by the user.
8. Caption: Made Mailloop for job seekers: personalized cold emails from your own Gmail, with resume attachments and delivery and reply tracking.
9. Flow: choose template and contacts → check personal preview and attach resume → queue individual emails and follow delivery/replies.

## The angle
One draft. Individual introductions. Show the product doing the work with a source-grounded Compose recreation. All names, companies and email content in the video are fictional demonstration data; use example.com addresses. No live account or actual email sending.

## Hook
“Write once.” / “Make every intro personal.” The second line is creative framing of the project's personalized outreach. A single email outline opens into the Compose screen.

## Key moments and outro
Select fictional recipients, then watch placeholder values become a personal preview. Resume attachment is selected explicitly. Transition into a simulated History view with Sent and Replied statuses. End on mailloop and the source tagline “Personalized outreach, made easy.” with “Sends from your Gmail · One email per person.”

## Storyboard
### 1 — Individual introductions — 0–3s
Large “Write once.” followed by “Make every intro personal.” Brand mark remains visible. Settled hook holds at least 1.5s. Soft crossfade to Compose; warm music begins. No sequential small print.

### 2 — Compose — 3–10s
Caption “Pick up to 15 contacts.” Source-derived workspace shows Compose, Templates, History, Contacts, Settings tabs; fictional recipient cards appear, selections fill one by one; template placeholders transform into a personal preview. Explicit “Attach Resume” selection, using resume.pdf. Screen is a stylized recreation, not a live session. Readable selected recipient names and large preview text; quiet selection sound. Natural animation timing with optional beat-grid alignment.

### 3 — Send, then follow — 10–16s
Caption “Send, then follow.” Simulate Send queue moving into History. Show a completion ring and example Sent / Replied status rows. Label “Demo · time compressed” so no suggestion of instant actual delivery or guaranteed replies. All results fictional. Status rows settle for several seconds. Completion accent near 13.11s. Soft transition.

### 4 — Mailloop — 16–20s
Logo reveal around 17.47s. “Personalized outreach, made easy.” Two short supports: “Sends from your Gmail” and “One email per person.” Hold fully visible through the end. Music fades gently.
